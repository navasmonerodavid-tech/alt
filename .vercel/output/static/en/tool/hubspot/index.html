📊 Pros & Cons

✅ Pros

  • + Truly free and very complete CRM
  • + All-in-one: marketing, sales and service
  • + Great partner and app ecosystem
  • + Free training academy

❌ Cons

  • - Gets expensive with advanced modules
  • - Limited reporting customization
  • - Steep learning curve using all tools
  • - Hard to migrate data to another CRM

🎯 Best For

SMBs starting with CRM wanting to scale to marketing automation without changing platforms.

⚠️ Not Ideal For

Not if you need pure sales tracking or have 1000+ employees with very specific needs.
