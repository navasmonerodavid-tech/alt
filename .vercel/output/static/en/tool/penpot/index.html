✓ Browser-based vector design and prototyping

📊 Pros & Cons

✅ Pros

  • + Completely free and open source
  • + Real CSS-based flex layout
  • + No artificial limits
  • + Clean SVG/PDF/PNG export

❌ Cons

  • - Fewer plugins than Figma
  • - Smaller community
  • - Some features still in beta
  • - Lower performance on large files vs Figma

🎯 Best For

Designers and teams seeking the open source Figma alternative with no price or feature limits.

⚠️ Not Ideal For

Not if you need very specific Figma plugins, advanced motion design or maximum smoothness in huge files.
