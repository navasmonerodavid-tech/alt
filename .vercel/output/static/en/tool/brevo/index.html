📊 Pros & Cons

✅ Pros

  • + Very generous free plan (300 emails/day)
  • + Includes SMS and CRM at no extra cost
  • + Contact-based, not email-based pricing
  • + Modern fast interface

❌ Cons

  • - Fewer templates than Mailchimp
  • - Less powerful automations
  • - Customer support could improve
  • - Fewer native integrations

🎯 Best For

SMBs wanting email marketing + SMS + CRM in one affordable platform with a good free plan.

⚠️ Not Ideal For

Not for large corporations with very complex automation or advanced multilingual needs.
