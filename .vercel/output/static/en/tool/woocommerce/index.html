📊 Pros & Cons

✅ Pros

  • + 100% free (base plugin)
  • + Full control over your store
  • + No per-sale commissions
  • + Infinitely customizable with plugins

❌ Cons

  • - Requires hosting and maintenance
  • - Needs WordPress knowledge
  • - Premium extensions can be expensive
  • - Performance depends on hosting

🎯 Best For

WordPress users wanting ecommerce with full control and no commissions, willing to manage their own hosting.

⚠️ Not Ideal For

Not for those unwilling to manage hosting or needing an all-in-one solution without technical setup.

Frequently asked questions

What are the downsides of using WooCommerce?+
Limitations in customization, additional costs for extensions, and performance issues are some downsides.
Which WooCommerce alternatives are easy to use?+
Some alternatives are easy to use and offer intuitive interfaces for setting up online stores.
What is the cost of migrating from WooCommerce to another platform?+
The cost varies depending on the store's complexity and the target platform.
Can I keep my products and customers when switching platforms?+
Yes, many platforms offer migration tools to transfer products and customers.
What features should I look for in a WooCommerce alternative?+
