📊 Pros & Cons

✅ Pros

  • + Full privacy with local encryption
  • + Very Notion-like experience
  • + Open source and free
  • + Works perfectly offline

❌ Cons

  • - Still in active development (frequent changes)
  • - Fewer integrations than Notion
  • - Smaller community
  • - Sync can be slow at times

🎯 Best For

Users wanting the Notion experience with real privacy, encryption and full data control.

⚠️ Not Ideal For

Not if you need advanced real-time collaboration or many external tool integrations.
