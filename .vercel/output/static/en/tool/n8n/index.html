📊 Pros & Cons

✅ Pros

  • + 100% open source and free (self-hosted)
  • + Unlimited executions
  • + Full data control
  • + Very intuitive visual editor

❌ Cons

  • - Requires a server (or paid cloud)
  • - 350 integrations vs 7,000 on Zapier
  • - Less support and documentation
  • - Updates can break workflows

🎯 Best For

Technical teams needing powerful automations with full control, no price limits and custom code.

⚠️ Not Ideal For

Not for non-technical users who cannot manage servers or need thousands of pre-built integrations.

For large enterprises, alternatives like Microsoft Power Automate or Apache Airflow may be more suitable due to their scalability and advanced features.
