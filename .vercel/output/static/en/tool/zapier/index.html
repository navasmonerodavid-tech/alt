📊 Pros & Cons

✅ Pros

  • + Largest integration library
  • + Very easy for non-technical users
  • + Functional free plan
  • + Large community support

❌ Cons

  • - Very expensive on higher plans
  • - Charges per task, not per flow
  • - Strict limits on free plan
  • - No true testing environment

🎯 Best For

Anyone needing to connect apps without code, ideal for simple to medium automations.

⚠️ Not Ideal For

Not for automations with hundreds of thousands of tasks/month (too expensive) or very complex workflows.
