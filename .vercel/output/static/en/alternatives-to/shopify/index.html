Frequently asked questions

What are the free alternatives to Shopify?+
Options like WooCommerce and OpenCart offer basic e-commerce functionalities for free.
What is the best Shopify alternative for small online stores?+
It depends on specific needs, but options like BigCommerce and Magento are popular among small sellers.
Can I migrate my Shopify store to another platform?+
Yes, most e-commerce platforms allow data migration, although it may require technical help.
What features should I look for in a Shopify alternative?+
You should consider factors like customization, costs, third-party integration, and customer support.
Are Shopify alternatives secure?+
Most alternative platforms offer robust security measures, but it's essential to research and compare before making a decision.
